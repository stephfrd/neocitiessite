Hello!! My name is Steph and on this side of my website, I'll try to write weekly logs of how my life goes on. This can be related to my coding projects or my personal life:3 Thank you for stopping by.

12-13 april 2026: I've been wanting to make a neocities website for a while, but I have never gotten around to it.I had enough time now and I decided to at least try and do somethinh. Some parts of the site are things that I have built before and I just copied the text to this website, some I did now, and learned a lot of things along the way.💜

24 may 2026: I haven't been here for a while because school has been kicking my ass, but i made a few tweaks to a few of my pages and im glad to see that a few people saw this website! i hope they liked it. In another order of events, I recently switched from arch linux to nixos and I must say...i've never done anything so easily before. I was a bit hesitant at first because it seemed more complicated, but it's actyally not that bad! In MY opinion, it's way better in terms of how many stuff I can download and how easily I can do it. I have also started learning Rust these past few weeks and it's been great! I have made a bit of progress and I'm looking forward to what I can do in the future in Rust. I also need to lock in for my Data Structures Class (we use C++) because it's honestly not going that well but I'm sure everything will be just fine! I finish the academic year in a month or so and I cannot wait! We aren't doing a lot, just tests and quizzes and stuff, so it's all pretty boring. I've been looking into making my own homelab!

8 july 2026: I did start my own homelab, as I intended to do. I have made a homepage for it, enabled talescale, navidrome, and plex. I'm having a bit of trouble downloading music because a lot of mp3 downloaders either don't work (and i really dont want a virus), or they just let me download a few songs (like 4) and then they want me to wait an hour, or even worse, a day. I've been occupying my summer break with making a homelab, as I mentioned, coding, doing calculus, reading and seeing movies. I want to learn a bit of machine learning, python and AI stuff this summer, but I don't know if I'll have the time because I will be gone the whole month of August. I watched Supergirl in cinemas and I thought it rocked!!

10 july 2026: Hello everyone!! I got 1k views on this site, which, to me, is amazing and i want to thank everyone who took the time to look at it!! I made a repo for my homelab, if anyone wants to see!! Also I would like to make a chatbox here or a message box or whatever you want to call it. I've been doing a lot of math and a lot of studying but on Monday (the 13th, I mean) I am going on a summer camp for two weeks, a coding summer camp, and I'm really excited about it!!
